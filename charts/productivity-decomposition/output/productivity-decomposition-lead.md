Productivity growth remains solid, but it now comes from businesses working their existing staff and equipment harder and from investment in computers, not from efficiency gains. Labor productivity grew 2.2 percent over the past four quarters. Higher utilization added 1.5 percentage points and computers and software 0.7. Total factor productivity, the best gauge of efficiency, subtracted 0.3 point, after adding as much as 3.3 points in 2023.
