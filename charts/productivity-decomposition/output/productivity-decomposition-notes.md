::: {.chart-notes}

**Other capital and labor:** Other equipment, buildings, and research per hour worked, plus a more educated and experienced workforce.

**Computers and software:** Computer and software capital per hour worked. Includes AI investment but is not limited to it.

**Total factor productivity:** Output growth not explained by capital, workforce skills, or utilization. The best gauge of efficiency gains.

**Utilization:** How intensively businesses use the workers and equipment they already have. Estimated from hours per worker.
