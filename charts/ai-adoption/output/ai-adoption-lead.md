AI use is spreading, but how widespread it looks depends on who is counted. About 24 percent of firms use AI, while 45 percent of workers use generative AI for their jobs. The gap is mostly about size: most firms are small, and large firms, which employ far more workers, are much more likely to use AI. One survey puts 78 percent of jobs at firms that use it. How the question is asked matters too: when the Census Bureau broadened its question in November 2025, the firm share jumped from 10 to 17 percent.
