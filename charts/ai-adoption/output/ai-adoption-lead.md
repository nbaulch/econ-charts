AI use is spreading, but how widespread it looks depends on who is counted and what is asked. About 24 percent of firms used AI in September 2026, against 45 percent of workers in May 2026, and one survey puts 78 percent of jobs at firms that use it. Large firms lead: 44 percent of firms with 250 or more employees use it, against 24 percent of those with fewer than five. Wording matters too: when the Census Bureau broadened its question in November 2025, the firm share jumped from 10 to 17 percent.
