AI use is spreading across businesses of every size, but it is concentrated in large firms. About 41 percent of firms with 250 or more employees now use it, against 23 percent of firms with fewer than 50. Because most firms are small, only about 24 percent of all firms use AI. Surveys of workers find wider use: 45 percent of employed adults use generative AI for their jobs, a broader measure that counts any use of tools such as chatbots.
