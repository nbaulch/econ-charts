::: {.chart-notes}

**Firms:** Share of businesses that used AI in the previous two weeks. Until October 2025 the question asked about AI in producing goods or services; since November 2025 it asks about AI in any business function.

**Workers:** Share of employed adults who use generative AI for their job.

**Jobs at firms using AI:** Share of employment at firms that use AI, from one survey of business executives in November 2025.
