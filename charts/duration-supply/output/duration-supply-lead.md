Treasury is not adding more long-term debt for private investors to absorb than it was a year ago; big tech borrowing is the new source. In the 12 months to August 2026, Treasury securities added $980 billion in 10-year equivalents to private holdings, compared with $1,004 billion a year earlier and a peak of $1,221 billion in December 2024. Bonds sold by Alphabet, Amazon, Meta, and Oracle added $284 billion, 29 percent as much. That understates big tech's borrowing, since it leaves out private placements such as the financing for Meta's Hyperion data center.
