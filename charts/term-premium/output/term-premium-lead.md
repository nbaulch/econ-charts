From September 2025 to September 18, 2026, the 10-year Treasury yield rose from 4.11 to 5.01 percent. The New York Fed's model attributes 0.80 point of the rise to higher expected short-term rates and 0.02 to the term premium. The Fed Board's model attributes 0.35 to expected rates and 0.50 to the term premium. Over two years, the models agree that the term premium rose 0.83 to 0.87 point.
