Underlying inflation is running between about 2.3 and 3.3 percent, depending on the measure, and every measure has picked up in recent months. Headline PCE prices rose 3.7 percent over the 12 months to July 2026, lifted by energy prices. Core prices, which leave out food and energy, rose 3.3 percent. Measures that set aside the biggest price swings run lower, down to 2.3 percent for the Dallas Fed's trimmed mean. Over the past six months, headline prices rose at a 4.1 percent annual rate and core at 3.5 percent.
