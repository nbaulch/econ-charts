Price increases have broadened since early 2025. In July 2026, prices of 54 percent of the 177 categories of consumer spending were up more than 3 percent from a year earlier, compared with 36 percent in January 2025. That is more than in any month from 2000 to 2020, when the high was 53 percent in 2008, though well below the 77 percent reached in July 2022. Kevin Warsh [pointed to this measure](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/warsh20260828a.htm) at Jackson Hole in August.
