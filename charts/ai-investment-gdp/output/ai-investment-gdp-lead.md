Investment in software, computers, data centers, and power—the spending that carries the AI buildout—has lifted real GDP growth since early 2025, but by less than headline figures suggest because many of the computers are imported. Over the past four quarters, this investment added 0.66 percentage point to real GDP growth of 2.1 percent. Net of imported computers and parts, it added 0.45 point, and as little as 0.34 under other reasonable assumptions. In 2023, before the buildout, it added about 0.3 point with or without imports.
