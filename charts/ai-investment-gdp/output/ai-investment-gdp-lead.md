Investment in software, computers, data centers, and power—the spending that carries the AI buildout—has lifted real GDP growth over the past two years, but by less than headline figures suggest because many of the computers are imported. Over the past four quarters, this investment added 0.66 percentage point to real GDP growth of 2.1 percent. Net of imported computers and parts, it added 0.45 point, and as little as 0.34 under other reasonable assumptions. In 2022 and 2023, before the buildout, the same investment added about 0.3 point, mostly from software.
