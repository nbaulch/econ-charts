AI investment has lifted real GDP growth over the past two years, but headline figures overstate the boost because much of the equipment is imported. Over the four quarters through 2026 Q2, AI-related investment added 0.66 percentage point to growth of 2.1 percent, about 80 percent of business investment's contribution. Net of imported computers, the boost was 0.45 point, and as little as 0.34 under other reasonable assumptions. In 2022 and 2023 it averaged about 0.3 point.
