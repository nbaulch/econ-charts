AI-related investment accounts for most of what business investment is adding to growth, but imported equipment offsets a third to a half of it. Over the four quarters through 2026 Q2, real GDP grew 2.1 percent at an annual rate, and business fixed investment added 0.84 percentage point. Software, computers, data centers, and power accounted for 0.66 point of that. Net of imported computers, their contribution falls to 0.45 point, or to 0.34 under other reasonable ways of counting computer trade.
