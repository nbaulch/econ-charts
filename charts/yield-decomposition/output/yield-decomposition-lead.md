The 10-year Treasury yield is about 3 percentage points higher than before the Fed began raising rates in 2022, and since the end of 2023 the term premium has done most of the rising. In the Fed Board's model, the yield averaged 0.72 point more in August 2026 than in December 2023. The term premium—the extra return investors require to lock up money for 10 years instead of rolling over short-term bills—accounted for 0.56 point of that and expected inflation 0.18, while expected real short-term rates were little changed.
